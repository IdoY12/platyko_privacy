This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices and rights you have. It is written to be read, not skimmed past — but if you only have a minute, start with the summary below.

No tracking, no ads

No analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind — in the app or on this website.

Your data is never sold

We do not sell personal data or share it with third parties for marketing. Ever.

Guest Mode is fully on-device

Use Platyko without an account — your progress stays only on your device, and nothing about you is stored on our servers.

Delete your account any time

Account deletion is built into the app’s Settings. It is immediate and permanent, with backup copies purged within 30 days.

01Who We Are

Platyko (“the app”) is developed and operated by Ido Yahav, an individual developer based in Israel, acting as the data controller for any personal data processed through the app and this website.

  • Operator: Ido Yahav (individual developer, no registered company), Israel
  • Contact: platyko.js@gmail.com
  • Website: platyko.com

This policy applies to the Platyko iOS app and to this website. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

02Information We Collect

We collect personal data only from registered users. If you use Platyko in Guest Mode, we collect nothing about you — see Section 04.

Account information

  • Email address and password — if you register with email, we store your email address and a hashed version of your password (using the bcrypt algorithm). We never store or have access to your plain-text password.
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple — if you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address and basic profile information via a secure identity token (id_token). We never receive or store a password for these accounts. With Sign in with Apple, you may choose to hide your email address, in which case we receive only Apple’s private relay address.
  • Profile avatar — if you choose to upload a profile picture, it is stored on Amazon Web Services (AWS S3).

Learning data

  • Lesson and course progress
  • Daily streaks
  • Duel (head-to-head challenge) history and results
  • Puzzle results and scores

Technical data

  • Push notification tokens — if you enable push notifications, we store a device push token (Expo push token / Apple Push Notification service token) so we can deliver notifications to your device.
  • Verification and reset codes — when you verify your email or reset your password, we generate a one-time code (OTP). Codes are stored in hashed form only and are automatically deleted after 10 minutes.

03What We Don’t Collect

Platyko is deliberately built without the data collection common in mobile apps. The app contains:

  • No analytics SDKs — we do not measure or profile how you use the app
  • No crash-reporting SDKs
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No tracking of any kind — no device fingerprinting, no cross-app identifiers, no location collection

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

04Guest Mode

You can use Platyko without creating an account. In Guest Mode:

  • Your learning progress is stored only locally on your device (in the app’s local storage). It never leaves your device.
  • The app makes anonymous, unauthenticated requests to our servers to fetch learning content (lessons, puzzles). These requests carry no account, profile, or identifier — nothing is stored on our servers or associated with you.
  • If you later create an account, your locally stored guest progress is uploaded to our servers and linked to your new account so you can continue where you left off. Until that moment, it remains exclusively on your device.
  • If you delete the app without creating an account, your guest progress is deleted with it.

05Children’s Privacy

Platyko is designed to be safe for young learners, and our children’s privacy model is simple: children can use the app, but we never collect their data.

  • The app is suitable for ages 6 and up in Guest Mode. As described in Section 04, Guest Mode stores progress only on the device and sends no personal information to our servers.
  • Creating an account requires users to be at least 13 years old, in line with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  • Because guest progress is only uploaded to our servers when an account is created — and account creation requires being 13 or older — we do not knowingly collect or store any personal data from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at platyko.js@gmail.com and we will delete the account and its data promptly.

06How We Use Information

We use the information described above solely to operate the app:

  • To create, authenticate, and secure your account
  • To save and sync your learning progress, streaks, duels, and puzzle results across sessions
  • To display your profile (including your avatar) to you and, where relevant, to duel opponents
  • To send push notifications you have enabled (for example, streak reminders or duel updates)
  • To send transactional emails: email verification codes and password-reset codes
  • To respond to your support and privacy requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making, and we do not sell it.

09International Data Transfers

Our servers are hosted on AWS in the United States (us-east-1 region). If you use Platyko from outside the United States — including from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Israel — your personal data is transferred to and stored in the United States.

For transfers from the EEA and UK, we rely on AWS’s Data Processing Addendum, which incorporates the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, as the safeguard for these transfers. AWS also participates in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account directly in the app: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent — your account, profile, avatar, learning progress, streaks, duel history, puzzle results, and push tokens are erased from our production systems right away, and any residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days.

You can also request deletion by emailing platyko.js@gmail.com from the address associated with your account.

11Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; we never store plain-text passwords
  • One-time verification and reset codes are stored hashed and expire after 10 minutes
  • All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Server access is restricted and data is hosted on AWS infrastructure

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we design the app to hold the minimum data necessary — the best protection for data we don’t need is not to collect it at all.

12Your Rights

Wherever you are, you can exercise the rights below by emailing platyko.js@gmail.com. We will verify your request (normally by confirming control of the account email) and respond within 30 days.

  • Access — receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — have inaccurate data corrected
  • Deletion — have your data erased (also available directly in-app)
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection & restriction — object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent — for example, disable push notifications at any time

European Economic Area & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

All of the rights above apply. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority if you believe our processing violates the GDPR.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know, access, correct, and delete personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. Platyko does not “sell” or “share” personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA/CPRA, and does not use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

Israel (Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981)

Under the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, you have the right to review data held about you in our database and to request correction or deletion of inaccurate data. You may also submit a complaint to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority.

13Push Notifications

Push notifications are strictly opt-in: the app only sends them if you grant permission through iOS. We use them for things like streak reminders and duel updates — never for advertising.

You can turn notifications off at any time in the app’s settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Platyko. When you delete your account, your push tokens are deleted with it.

14Emails We Send

We send transactional emails only, delivered via AWS SES:

  • Email verification codes when you register
  • Password-reset codes when you request them

We do not send marketing, promotional, or newsletter emails, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from.

15This Website

platyko.com is a static website. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and includes no trackers or third-party scripts. Visiting this page sends us no personal information.
