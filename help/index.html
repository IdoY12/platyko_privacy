01Getting Started

What is Platyko?

Platyko is a JavaScript learning app for iOS. You learn through structured lessons, sharpen your skills with puzzles, and test yourself against other learners in 1v1 duels. It’s free to use, with no ads and no tracking.

Do I need an account to use Platyko?

No. You can start learning immediately in Guest Mode — just open the app and go. In Guest Mode, your progress is stored only on your device and nothing about you is sent to our servers.

What’s the difference between Guest Mode and an account?

  • Guest Mode — no sign-up needed. Your progress lives only on your device, so it can’t follow you to a new device, and it’s deleted if you delete the app.
  • With an account — your progress, streaks, and duel history are saved to our servers, so they survive reinstalls and device changes. An account is also what lets you compete in duels with a profile of your own.

Already made progress as a guest? It isn’t lost. When you register, the progress stored on your device is uploaded and linked to your new account, so you continue exactly where you left off.

02Accounts

How do I create an account?

You can register with an email address and password, or sign in with Google or Apple. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account — younger learners can use Guest Mode instead.

How does email verification work?

When you register with email, we send a one-time verification code to your inbox. A few things to know:

  • If the email doesn’t arrive within a minute or two, check your spam or junk folder
  • Codes expire after 10 minutes — if yours has expired, just request a new one
  • You can resend the code after 60 seconds if it hasn’t arrived

Can I sign in with Google or Apple?

Yes. Signing in with Google or Apple skips the password entirely — we receive only your email address and basic profile information through a secure identity token, and no password is ever created or stored for these accounts. With Sign in with Apple, you can also choose to hide your email address.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Use the Forgot password option on the sign-in screen. We’ll email you a one-time reset code — it works the same way as verification codes (check spam, codes expire after 10 minutes, resend available after 60 seconds). Note that accounts created with Google or Apple don’t have a password to reset — just sign in with that provider again.

03Learning & Progress

How do lessons work?

Lessons are structured, bite-sized steps that take you through JavaScript from the ground up. Your progress through lessons and courses is saved as you go — on your device in Guest Mode, or synced to your account if you’re registered.

What are puzzles?

Puzzles are standalone challenges that let you practice what you’ve learned. Your puzzle results and scores are saved alongside your lesson progress.

How do daily streaks work?

Your streak counts the consecutive days you’ve met your daily goal. Learn every day and it grows; miss a day and it resets to zero. Streaks are a nudge to build a small, consistent habit — a little JavaScript every day beats a marathon once a month.

What is the daily goal?

The daily goal is the amount of learning that counts as “showing up” for the day. Completing it keeps your streak alive. If you’ve enabled notifications, the app can remind you before the day runs out.

04Duels

How do 1v1 duels work?

Duels are head-to-head JavaScript challenges against another learner. You both face the same questions — answer correctly and quickly to win. Your duel history and results are saved to your account.

What counts as fair play?

Duels only work if everyone competes on the same terms. That means no automated solvers, no using someone else’s answers, and no manipulating scores, streaks, or rankings. The full rules are in Terms of Use §04 — Acceptable Use; breaking them can lead to account suspension or termination.

If you think you’ve found a bug that could be exploited, please don’t use it — report it to platyko.js@gmail.com instead.

05Notifications

How do I turn notifications on or off?

Notifications are strictly opt-in — the app only sends them if you grant permission through iOS. We use them for things like streak reminders and duel updates, never for advertising. You can change your mind at any time:

  • In the app — toggle notifications in the app’s settings
  • In iOS — go to Settings → Notifications → Platyko and turn them off there

If notifications aren’t arriving even though you want them, check both places — iOS settings override the app.

06Privacy & Data

The short version: no analytics, no ads, no tracking — and Guest Mode collects nothing at all. The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

What data does Platyko collect?

Only what’s needed to run your account: your email address, a hashed password (if you register with email), your optional avatar, and your learning data — lesson progress, streaks, duel history, and puzzle results. There are no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs in the app. See Privacy Policy §02 for the full list.

Is Guest Mode really private?

Yes. In Guest Mode your progress is stored only on your device, and the app’s requests to fetch lessons and puzzles carry no account or identifier. Nothing about you is stored on our servers until the moment you choose to register. See Privacy Policy §04.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no grace period or recovery. You can also request deletion by emailing platyko.js@gmail.com from the address associated with your account.

What happens to my data after I delete my account?

Your account, profile, avatar, learning progress, streaks, duel history, puzzle results, and push tokens are erased from our production systems right away. Any residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. See Privacy Policy §10.

07Troubleshooting

The app isn’t working properly. What should I try?

  • Close the app fully and reopen it
  • Check that you have a working internet connection — lessons and puzzles are fetched from our servers
  • Make sure you’re on the latest version of Platyko in the App Store
  • Restart your device

Still stuck? Email us at platyko.js@gmail.com with a description of what’s happening and, if possible, your device model and iOS version.

If you’re in Guest Mode, be careful about deleting the app while troubleshooting — guest progress is stored only on your device and is deleted with the app. Creating an account first will save your progress to our servers.

I didn’t get my verification email

  • Check your spam or junk folder — verification emails sometimes land there
  • Double-check that you typed your email address correctly
  • Wait at least 60 seconds, then use the resend option in the app
  • Remember that each code expires after 10 minutes — if you’ve waited longer, request a fresh one

If the email still doesn’t arrive after several tries, contact us at platyko.js@gmail.com.

My avatar won’t upload

  • Check your internet connection and try again
  • Try a smaller or different image — a standard photo or square image works best
  • Make sure Platyko has permission to access the photo you selected (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos)

Also note that avatars must be appropriate for a learning app used by teenagers, and avatars that break the rules may be removed — see Terms of Use §05.
